Chess remains one of the world's oldest and most intellectually challenging strategy games. Chess is believed to have originated in india around the 6th century ce, where an early game called chaturanga was played. The name comes from sanskrit and refers to the four divisions of an ancient indian army: infantry, cavalry, elephants, and chariots. From india, the game travelled to persia, where it became known as shatranj. Following the arab conquest of persia, chess spread throughout the islamic world and eventually reached europe. As chess travelled across different civilizations, its rules, pieces, and playing styles gradually changed. From royal courts to international tournaments, chess has stood the test of time as a symbol of intelligence, strategy, patience, and mastery. Today, it continues to unite millions of players across the world in an endless battle of minds.
